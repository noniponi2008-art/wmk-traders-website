About WMK Traders

A dependable partner for diverse business needs

WMK Traders began trading in 2019 and supplies electrical items, hardware, furniture, IT equipment, stationery and clothing.

We aim to simplify sourcing: you tell us what you need, we source it, and we keep you informed. Our work rests on prompt replies, being open about what we can and cannot supply, and attention to quality.

Run by Himanshu Taneja, who also provides training.

Our Operating Principle: Direct accountability and straightforward commercial communication on every order — from single-unit requirements to ongoing institutional consignments.

Operational Workflow

How we work

Our sourcing method is structured to remove guesswork, ensure verified quality, and keep procurement timelines reliable.

01

Share your requirements

Tell us item specifications, desired brands, approximate volume, and delivery destination in Karnal or surrounding regions.

02

Clear feasibility & quote

We check verified supply lines, confirm lead times, and provide an all-inclusive, transparent quotation with no hidden charges.

03

Inspection & dispatch

Materials are checked against your exact specifications before being packed and dispatched with proper transport transit coordination.

04

Invoicing & support

Complete GST documentation, MSME verification details, and post-delivery follow-up to ensure seamless institutional records.

Government & Enterprise Procurement

Government e-Marketplace (GeM) & Institutional Supply

WMK Traders actively supports government departments, educational institutions, universities, hospitals, and commercial corporate facilities across Haryana.

  • GeM Registered Vendor

    Fully registered on the Government e-Marketplace portal for compliant direct purchase, L1 quotes, and custom bid participation.

  • Udyam Registered MSME

    Recognized under the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME), Government of India.

  • GST Compliant Documentation

    Proper tax invoices with accurate HSN codes, e-way bills, and clear payment records for public and private auditing.

  • Regional Logistics Network

    Direct delivery capability throughout Karnal, Panipat, Kurukshetra, Kaithal, Ambala, and the NCR trade corridor.
